Kill criteria, written before the buy

  • Azeus. Contract liabilities at 31 Mar 2027 grow under 15%, or the CERKS licence dispute is lost. Either fires: exit.
  • iFAST. Two quarters of net outflows, or the Global Bank slips back into loss, or Hong Kong ePension revenue runs off with no replacement disclosed.
  • Kimly. Outlet count falls, net margin under 8%, or dividend uncovered by free cash flow.
  • Vicom (if struck). Inspection volumes fall two halves running, or the parent proposes a take-private below S$2.
  • Hongkong Land. Buyback paused, Central office vacancy rises two halves running, or gearing above 20%.
  • Haw Par. Tiger Balm revenue falls again in FY26, or the family deploys the cash pile into anything other than dividends or a buyback.
  • Wee Hur. Fund II leasing under 85%, a failed capital raise, or PATMI ex-one-offs declining.
  • UOB (if struck). Fully phased-in CET1 under 13.5%, or group NPL above 2%.
  • Thakral. DJI distribution lost, or GemLife falls more than 30% from its 30 Jun mark.
  • Boustead. FY27 Geospatial operating profit under S$40m, or Esri exclusivity not renewed.
